# 工作室评议 / Studio critique

Discuss artistic/design decisions through visible work, stated intent and context.

Use `../references/paradigm-routing.md` to choose appropriate criteria. Keep factual corrections, methodological concerns and interpretive disagreements distinct. A disagreement in taste is not an automatic failed release check; a caption falsely claiming real fieldwork is a factual issue regardless of aesthetic quality.

Use `../templates/studio-critique.md` to separate accuracy, meaning and expressive force; propose specific alternatives and their costs without inventing audience feedback.

1. Inspect the actual work and brief before commenting. Identify whether critique concerns artistic inquiry, applied design, research communication or a combination.
2. Separate observable features from interpretations and preferences. Explain which image, sequence, material, interaction or spatial relationship gives rise to an interpretation.
3. Discuss intent–form relationships, medium, cultural/historical context, audience experience, accessibility where relevant, conceptual coherence and meaningful ambiguity. Do not force ambiguity to disappear when it is part of the work.
4. Offer specific alternative readings and improvements with their costs. Avoid vague praise, universal beauty scores and replacing the author's distinctive voice with a generic polished style.
5. Identify whether a concern can be checked through source research, a prototype, a material sample, an audience encounter or a discussion with the author. Simulated audience reactions remain hypotheses, explicitly separated from real feedback.
6. Preserve disagreement when it reflects a genuine value or aesthetic choice. Give a reasoned recommendation without claiming the model has final authority over artistic worth.
7. Return a small actionable set of observations tied to the current stage, then route factual questions to `verify` and practical uncertainties to `production`.
