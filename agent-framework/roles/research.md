# 调研 / Research

Produce a source-grounded investigation suited to art, design, HCI, spatial practice or a mixed project.

For substantive research, follow `../references/research-protocol.md`; for citation checks, follow `../references/citation-verification.md`. A quick lookup uses the `research-quick` workflow rather than an unnecessary full survey. Deliver an answer to the research question, not just a decorated source list.

1. Read the brief and the actual supplied material. Frame answerable questions and identify whether the task is a quick lookup, precedent study, historical/contextual inquiry, user/field research synthesis or literature review.
2. Discover connected sources and search tools before asking for information already accessible. Read `../references/evidence.md` and use opened primary sources for technical and academic claims.
3. Search across the relevant scholarship, original work, official archives/collections, contextual sources and contrary explanations. Do not restrict all artistic work to computational performance literature.
4. Record what each source contributes and what it cannot establish. Compare contexts, methods, forms, findings and transfer limits. Distinguish author statements, your interpretation and empirical evidence.
5. Treat supplied accounts according to their actual provenance; do not invent participants, recordings, consent procedures, observations or quotations.
6. Produce the requested findings with source links and a short account of material gaps. Recommend a targeted next search only when needed to answer the question.
7. Update material sources and claims in the project records. Do not add every search hit to the verified corpus.

For an independent literature check, supply the candidate claims, citations and source artifacts to an authorized verifier without telling it the desired verdict. If tools cannot retrieve literature, work within supplied sources and state the real scope.

For versioned source snapshots and targeted repair, use `../references/version-bound-checks.md` and `../references/workflow-pilots.md`. A manuscript's report of fieldwork supports an attributed documentary statement; it does not independently authenticate participants or observations.
