# 写作与反防御表达 / Writing and anti-defensive expression

Draft, translate, polish or restructure the requested prose while preserving the user's evidence and voice.

Select the authorized operation through `../references/writing-modes.md`: planning, drafting, light polish, translation, restructuring or final delivery. For a full manuscript or major rewrite, build a separate argument blueprint and evidence map before prose. Use `../references/citation-verification.md` for citation checks and record the actual verification method rather than implying an independent pass.

1. Read `../policies/anti-defensive-integration.md` and the relevant preserved original skill before planning or editing prose. Keep that skill as the user's expression policy throughout, not merely a final word replacement pass.
2. Read the current source text and relevant source material. Identify genre, audience, project method, target language, authorized editing intensity and protected wording. A review-only task does not authorize edits.
3. For new prose, identify the paragraph's claim, supporting material, reasoning and contribution. For polishing, preserve facts, number ranges, terminology, uncertainty, causal direction, argument and authorial tone. Do not force all design or art writing into a technical six-paragraph introduction or IMRaD template.
4. Build the strongest accurate argument. State what the work examines, proposes, shows or enables directly. Remove ritual modesty, redundant self-negation, repeated scope disclaimers and process narration that does not serve the requested genre.
5. Resolve evidence gaps through research, a supported narrower statement or a separate author note. Never invent interviews, methods, mechanisms, outcomes, precedents or citations to make a paragraph feel complete.
6. Preserve necessary qualifiers once in the right place. Do not turn a design proposal into an evaluated outcome, an unresolved account into authenticated testimony, or a plausible interpretation into a factual finding.
7. Match Chinese and English propositions, terminology and claim strength when pairing languages. Follow an explicit artifact-language instruction over the bilingual default for that artifact.
8. Return clean usable prose and a short change note when needed. Keep verification findings and unresolved material questions outside final prose unless they must be stated to preserve meaning.

If the logic is broken, repair the authorized argument scope before polishing sentences; if the core claim is unsupported, surface the issue rather than rhetorically rescuing it. Treat user-provided working titles and distinctive expressions as intentional until a specific clarity problem justifies a proposed change.

Use `../references/local-protection.md` for locked wording and an outside-scope difference report. Review factual accuracy, authorial meaning and rhetorical strength separately, as in `../references/workflow-pilots.md`; stronger wording must remain supported, and necessary conditions need not become repeated disclaimers.
