# 核心流程的执行约定 / Core workflow execution contracts

调研先用问题矩阵比较材料的判断、根据和差异，再形成直接回答；来源摘录不能伪装成新完成的访谈或现场验证。
Research compares statements, bases and differences through a question matrix before answering directly; supplied excerpts cannot become newly conducted interviews or field validation.

分析图先区分已记录关系、解释、提议和实测几何，固定位向与连线范围，生成可编辑源文件后实际打开导出图像检查。
Analytical diagrams distinguish recorded relationships, interpretation, proposals and measured geometry, protect orientation and connections, and inspect an opened export after producing editable sources.

册子与论文核查先建立“主张—论文位置—册子位置—必要条件”表，分开记录 PDF 页序、书内页码和图号，检查可能在相邻页恢复的条件。
Booklet–thesis review begins with a claim-to-location-and-condition table, distinguishes PDF indices, printed pages and figure numbers, and checks whether adjacent pages restore a condition.

先查会改变含义的遗漏、增加和矛盾，再讨论压缩程度；核查请求只产生报告，种入错误的副本必须明确标注为测试材料。
Check meaning-changing omissions, additions and contradictions before judging compression; review requests produce reports, and seeded-error copies must be identified as test material.

写作分别检查事实准确、作者原意和表达力度，让成立的贡献成为主线，保留决定结论的条件；不通过压低语言力度来代替证据核查。
Writing separately reviews factual accuracy, authorial meaning and rhetorical strength, foregrounds supported contributions and retains consequential conditions; weak wording is not a substitute for evidence review.

工作室评议讨论作品意图、形式关系、语境、体验和替代解释，并指出下一次制作能够回答的问题，不把风格喜好包装成统一审美分数。
Studio critique addresses intent, formal relationships, context, experience and alternative interpretations, identifies questions for the next iteration, and does not turn taste into a universal beauty score.

当前八个试用案例及覆盖范围记录在 `project/evaluation/report.md`，它们是同一主 Agent 的有界试用与已知错误测试，不是盲测、独立用户研究或普遍性能证明。
The eight current pilot cases and coverage are recorded in `project/evaluation/report.md`; they are bounded same-agent trials and known-error tests, not blinded evaluation, independent user research or general performance evidence.
