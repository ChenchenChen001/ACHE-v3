# 方法与项目类型 / Methods and project types

Choose standards from the work's core contribution and method. A spatial project may combine creative inquiry, fieldwork, material production and technical evaluation; do not use one rubric for every part.

| Practice | Appropriate questions | Appropriate material | Do not impose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Artistic inquiry | What does the work propose, question or make perceptible? How do form, medium and context carry that intention? | Works, process studies, artist statements, archives, critique and documented encounters | Universal beauty scores, mandatory problem-solving, algorithm baselines |
| Applied design | Whose situation is addressed, under what constraints? Does the proposal support the intended use? | Context research, actual observations, prototypes, use tests, dimensions, cost and making records | Renderings as proof of performance or generalized claims from one user |
| Research-led design | What question is answered or reframed? How does practice produce a defensible contribution? | Traceable materials, explicit methods, annotated artifacts, comparative analysis and critical reflection | A technical six-paragraph introduction or IMRaD for every paper |
| Technical/HCI component | What function or mechanism is claimed, and what test can support it? | Actual task evidence, comparisons and controls appropriate to that component | Using a successful prototype to establish a broader social effect |

For mixed projects, record the standard for each major claim or artifact. A seating prototype can establish dimensions or interaction observations without establishing reduced loneliness. Artistic ambiguity can be intentional while factual captions remain precise.

Adapt the upstream humanities dimensions to conceptual contribution, method–question fit, material discovery, explanatory or experiential insight, and situated significance. Add resources and feasibility where material. Give numerical ratings only when useful and explicitly requested; do not present a simulated acceptance label as a prediction of a real jury or venue.

Separate an untested proposition from a proposition contradicted by supplied evidence. Do not reject exploration merely because evaluation has not happened. If an empirical central claim is contradicted, expose the contradiction and reframe the authorized proposal rather than writing a defense unsupported by the material.
