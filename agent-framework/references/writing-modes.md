# 写作模式与论证链 / Writing modes and argument structure

## Select the operation

| Operation | Input | Output | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan | Question, materials, intended contribution | Argument blueprint and evidence gaps | Do not claim finished prose or missing research |
| Draft | Author's substance plus traceable sources | Usable paragraph, section or manuscript | No invented procedures, cases, interviews, mechanisms or results |
| Polish | Existing text and authorized scope | Clearer text with the same propositions | Do not silently change meaning, claim strength, title or argument |
| Translate | Source text, terminology and target language | Meaning-matched idiomatic text | Do not invent causal links or strengthen certainty during translation |
| Restructure | Whole relevant argument and explicit authorization | Reorganized text plus consequential change map | Keep protected content and evidence boundaries |
| Final | Current complete artifact and requested requirements | Clean deliverable with actual verification coverage | Do not mark unperformed checks complete |

Use the preserved anti-defensive rules in every applicable writing operation. They govern expression and narrative, not source authenticity or permission to alter the user's argument.

## Plan before prose

For a full manuscript or major rewrite, produce a separate working evidence map and argument blueprint using `templates/argument-blueprint.md`. For a small fragment, perform the same check at an appropriate scale without forcing separate files.

Each argument unit has a question, claim or proposition, supporting material, reasoning, role, and bounded local conclusion. Trace promised contributions to the sections, figures, artifacts or observations that actually deliver them. For creative propositions, trace the formal/material exploration; do not demand an experiment unless an empirical effectiveness claim is made.

Check the chain: motivation or tension → question → approach → material/observations → interpretation or design proposition → contribution. A new module, case or section must perform a real role. Do not invent a limitation or challenge merely to justify an existing solution. Permit one-to-many relations when reasoned and explicit.

## Draft and preserve voice

Before each paragraph, settle what it can honestly say and why. A complete source does not support every claim; a missing source is resolved by retrieval, a supported narrower statement, removal from factual prose, or a separately recorded unresolved item. Planned or imagined results remain plans or propositions, not “preliminary findings.”

Lead with the supported point; retain the author's strongest defensible contribution. Avoid repetitive caveats, empty modesty and imagined objections. Preserve distinctive meaning-bearing wording, justified confident statements, actual failed trials and necessary conditions. Do not mechanically delete words because they appear on an AI-tone list.

For light polishing, make language-only edits freely within the authorized scope. Leave a meaning-changing candidate unchanged or provide it as a clearly identified proposal with a before/after explanation. Existing authorization for substantive rewriting can cover it; do not ask again for routine choices already authorized. Do not silently “fix” an unsupported conclusion through a change presented as mere grammar correction.

## Verify and deliver

Apply `citation-verification.md` to cited factual prose. In final prose, resolve evidence problems without editorial placeholder tags; keep the working evidence map, uncertainty and verification findings separate. Never hide a contradiction or convert a missing test into a success story.

Deliver the requested text first, then only material change notes or limits. A local edit does not establish whole-paper coherence. Chinese and English versions must carry the same scope, attribution and certainty.
