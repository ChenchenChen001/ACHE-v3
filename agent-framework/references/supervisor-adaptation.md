## 采用与调整 / Adopted and adapted

| Upstream method | ACHE adaptation |
|---|---|
| Deep research: brief, perspectives, synthesis, repair | Question-led research with targeted supplementary search; no fixed source quota and no forced mutually exclusive taxonomy for art |
| Paper writer: evidence map, blueprint, source discipline | Specific claim–source links and contribution tracing; full-text availability never supports arbitrary claims |
| Paper polish: fidelity to meaning | Separate plan/draft/polish/translate modes; keep authorial voice and flag meaning-risk edits without overriding existing authorization |
| Citation ladder | Record actual verification method; distinguish not-found from failed search; fresh-context work remains subject to authorization |
| Tech/intro consistency checks | Adapt to question, approach, material, interpretation and contribution; allow justified many-to-many relations |
| Figure design and visual audit | Visual brief, editable source and actual output inspection; allow photography/collage/raster media and project-specific typography |
| Reviewer severity | Quote actual artifacts and inspect counter-evidence; severity follows consequences, not universal word or format bans |
| Paradigm routing | Artistic, applied, research-led and technical components use appropriate evidence and critique standards |

## 未直接移植的规则 / Rules not transferred literally

- Do not require every introduction to contain six paragraphs or every contribution list to contain a fixed number of items.
- Do not require all visuals to be vector graphics or treat an em dash as a major research defect.
- Do not treat any retrieved full text as support for any claim; inspect the relevant passage and its conditions.
- Do not relabel planned results as preliminary results, even with cautious verbs.
- Do not assume that one study always merits only weak wording or multiple studies always merit strong wording; evaluate actual design, scope and agreement.
- Do not equate empty searches with novelty, infrastructure errors with nonexistent references, simulated critique with real audience feedback, or configuration checks with substantive validation.
