# 作品评议 / Studio critique

- 意图与现有形式：作者想让人感受或理解什么，哪些可见选择在承担这一作用？
  Intent and current form: what should people feel or understand, and which visible choices support it?
- 准确性：哪些事实、文字、空间关系或材料标识能够核实，是否存在具体错误？
  Accuracy: which facts, words, spatial relationships or material labels can be checked, and what specific errors exist?
- 意义与解释：当前形式支持哪些阅读，哪些解释与作者意图有张力？
  Meaning and interpretation: which readings does the current form support, and where do they diverge from intent?
- 表达力度：贡献、视觉重心和叙事节奏是否清楚，哪些重复解释可以删除？
  Rhetorical strength: are the contribution, visual focus and narrative rhythm clear, and which explanations are redundant?
- 下一轮制作：给出两个具体改变、各自的代价，以及真实测试能回答的问题。
  Next iteration: propose two concrete changes, their trade-offs and questions an actual test could answer.

将事实问题、合理分歧和个人偏好分开，不汇总成一个“美感总分”，也不把 AI 评议写成真实观众反馈。
Separate factual defects, reasonable disagreement and preference without a total beauty score or presenting AI critique as real audience feedback.
